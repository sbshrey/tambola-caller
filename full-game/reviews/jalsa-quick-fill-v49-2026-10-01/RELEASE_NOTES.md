# Tambola Jalsa · Beta v49 — 10-second quick tables

Tambola and Bingo quick matches now start after a 10-second countdown. Real people can join during that window; the server fills remaining seats with computer players at the deadline so early testers can play even when the beta has few people online. Computer players use the existing named avatars and play under the same calls and prize rules. The table UI labels them as Computer, and the roster distinguishes people from computer seats.

Friends tables still require invited people and an explicit host start. Existing v47/v48 waiting rooms retain their original human-only setting until they complete or refund; new quick purchases use the updated behavior.

This update needs the PC game service to be online. Coins are virtual, with no cash prizes or payments. Android asks before installing the compatible APK update.
