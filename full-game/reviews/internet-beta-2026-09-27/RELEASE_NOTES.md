# Tambola Internet Beta · alpha24

Play the Game night multiplayer game over mobile data or any Wi-Fi connection.

1. Download **Tambola-Internet-Beta-0.24.0.apk** below on an Android 8+ phone.
2. Open the APK and allow installation from your browser if Android asks.
3. Open **Tambola Internet Beta**, choose 1–6 tickets and tap **Play**. Friends should tap Play around the same time to enter the same open table. Empty seats use clearly labelled computers.

You receive 1,500 free virtual coins; tickets cost 100 each. Mark your tickets manually, use the arrows for more tickets, and claim prizes beside the matching ticket. Coins cannot be purchased, transferred or redeemed for money.

This is a separate app/profile from the earlier Wi-Fi build. Keep it installed to retain its saved player credentials and wallet. It does not require the old Wi-Fi APK or a certificate installation. English, Hindi and Hinglish caller clips are bundled.

The game is temporarily hosted on the developer's PC. The PC must remain awake, signed in and online. A connection interruption or tunnel restart can take a few minutes to recover; the app finds the new address automatically. This small-group beta uses development signing and a free tunnel, and has not been accepted for physical-phone performance or Store release.

Source and operating details: [Internet beta guide](https://github.com/sbshrey/tambola-caller/blob/codex/internet-beta/docs/INTERNET_BETA.md).
