# Tambola Jalsa · Beta v44 — Tambola and Bingo

Choose Tambola or 75-ball Bingo from one shared home screen, with the same player profile and coin wallet.

- Online and practice Bingo with one to six cards and bottom paging arrows.
- Public Bingo tables and private friends tables with a separate Bingo code.
- One line, four corners, X and full-card prizes, with remaining claim counts and same-call ties.
- Saved online tables, safe retries after interrupted purchases or marks, and reconnect support.
- Ranked round results, your own row highlighted, and Play again with your preferred card count.
- Portrait and landscape play, Hindi labels and support for larger text.

This update keeps the existing Android package, signing identity and saved profile. Install it through the existing in-app updater and confirm Android's installation prompt. Practice is available offline; online tables require the room server.

Retains the clearer Tambola play screen: called numbers above tickets, Tickets / Board tabs, circular prize-progress controls and confirmed-win confetti.
