The optimized alpha21 APK stayed installed unchanged on the dedicated API30 emulator. The external driver was built from `abef7138f0ed35cc44fae44a049dbd6d8a542d34`; [preflight identities](preflight.json) and [post-run verification](validation.json) identify both installed APKs and the Wi-Fi service runtime. The runner exited **1**; this attempt is not an endurance pass.

Round three deliberately killed and reopened the app process. Completed-round durations total **3,232,900 ms (53.88 minutes)**, excluding trace-processing gaps. The [failure capture](failure.png) shows normal gameplay at call 23 with the picker absent; the saved progress counter was last updated at call 20. `ClaimArena` deliberately dismisses the picker on a new call or round completion. This evidence is consistent with the captured picker becoming stale across that boundary; a focused native regression is required to verify the driver repair. The [full JUnit transcript](native-transcript.txt) retains the failure. No app-crash or full-run success is inferred from this test-driver exception.

## Driver repair and focused regression

The repaired driver takes a fresh public snapshot after a missing, disabled or stale prize choice. It tolerates that observation only when the call or active phase changed. It does not blindly click again, and it checks any already-accepted award before continuing. Missing/disabled controls within the same active call still fail; a same-call `StaleObjectException` is rethrown.

The first focused probe waited for the server's first call but reached a still-disabled native Claim control. Its [failure and cleanup](picker-probe-initial/coin-release-journey.json) are retained. The corrected probe waits for the actual app control to become enabled, captures a picker object, waits for the next normal call to dismiss it, and deliberately performs the original stale enabled-state read before exercising the repair.

The [corrected regression](driver-repair/native-transcript.txt) passed in **41.058 seconds** on the unchanged optimized alpha21 app. It reproduced the original stale read across calls **1 → 2**, recorded one handled call rollover, verified no claim had been submitted and reopened/dismissed a fresh picker. Both probe attempts removed their four profiles. [Driver identity and source hashes](driver-repair/validation.json) identify the installed driver `671fdc9c4ad5d903d84938d6240de0d03568f79b83749f6ea284479b12e6ba73`; the app still matched its original alpha21 bytes. The runner confirmed restored animation settings and no adb reverse mappings remained. The short regression's frame samples are not a gameplay performance result.

This remains one software emulator with three passive human QA peers. It does not validate real human competition, computer-opponent gameplay, physical Wi-Fi or ARM64 performance. No controlled HTTP-response loss was injected. A complete rerun, the queued UI/server checks and the remaining release gates are still required.
