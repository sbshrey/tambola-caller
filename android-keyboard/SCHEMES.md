# The 41 prize choices

In the keyboard, tap **Winners → Choose prize schemes**. Choose a group, tick the prizes, then **Add selected**. Or choose **Add all 41 schemes** and confirm to turn on the full list.

New prizes start at **₹10 total**. One winner receives ₹10; two winners receive ₹5 each. Change amounts or names under **Players & prizes**. Selection works before or during a game and keeps current calls, players, prizes and winners. Existing matching prizes are reused, with their names, amounts and awards preserved. The old Top/Middle/Bottom line and Full house entries also stay unless you turn them off.

These names came from the host's supplied list. Obvious spelling and spacing were tidied; the app does not assign definitions to regional prize names. The host checks each paper ticket using the group's agreed rules. Early 5 and lines require at least 5 calls, Early 10 requires 10, and Houses require 15. Other named presets can be recorded after the first call; this is not automatic ticket validation. Existing custom entries retain their original minimum-call setting when reused.

Up to 60 schemes can be stored, including the existing defaults and additional custom prizes. A new game keeps your chosen list and amounts and clears its winners. The website's prize setup is separate; this catalog is included in Android Keyboard v1.2.0.
