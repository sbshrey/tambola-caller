Tambola Keyboard Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy applies to Tambola Keyboard for Android, published from the sbshrey/tambola-caller repository.

Information used by the app

Tambola Keyboard has no account, advertising, analytics, or app-operated server. It does not request Internet, contacts, or accessibility permission. Player names, game progress, prize settings, and voice preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to the developer. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for this data. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Keyboard access

When you enable and select Tambola Keyboard, Android connects it to the focused text field. Like other keyboards, it can interact with that field while active. The app checks the current selection and nearby draft text to avoid replacing a message, inserts text you choose, and can remove its own unsent number when you use Undo. It does not save chat drafts or build a history of text entered in other apps. Android password fields, non-text fields, and Tambola's own setup fields use your regular keyboard.

Optional winner dictation

If you choose “Speak prize + winner,” Android asks for microphone permission. Recording starts only after you tap the speech control in the keyboard. The app sends a recognition request through Android's configured speech recognition service and receives a candidate transcription to identify a prize and winner. Depending on that service and your settings, audio may be processed on your device or transmitted to the service provider. The provider's own privacy policy and processing practices apply. Offline recognition is not guaranteed. Tambola Keyboard does not keep microphone recordings or a transcript history. Names recognized as winners are saved with your local game data.

Winner dictation is optional. You can deny microphone access and choose winners manually.

Sharing

When you tap a share or insert control, the app prepares the message, picture, or audio clip you selected and hands it to the Android text or file sharing system. You choose the receiving app and recipient and confirm sending there. The app does not send anything automatically and cannot confirm delivery. Temporary files prepared for sharing are kept in app-private storage. Files older than 48 hours are cleaned up when another share is prepared; receiving apps may keep their own copies.

Retention and security

Game and settings data remain in Android's app-private storage until you clear app data or uninstall. Temporary shared files are cleaned up when another share is prepared, including files older than 48 hours. The app uses Android's private app storage and does not operate a service that receives this data. Protect your device with its normal screen-lock and security settings.

Children

Tambola Keyboard does not request age information. It has no advertising or analytics SDKs. A parent or guardian should supervise microphone use and sharing when a child uses the device.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date above will be revised with any update. For privacy questions, use the repository issue tracker. It is public by default, so do not include private or sensitive information.
